COMBE DOWN WITH ME

Three minutes, one kitchen, and a queue out of the door

How to play

Orders come in at the top. Fetch food from the crates at the back and front, get it ready, put it on a plate and hand it through the pass under the heat lamp. Loaves, cheddar and tomatoes are chopped on a board; bacon and eggs go on a hob; Bath bun dough goes in the oven. Leave anything on the heat too long and it burns: bin it. The quicker a dish goes out, the bigger the tip, and anyone who waits too long walks out.
