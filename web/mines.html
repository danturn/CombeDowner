How to play

Three levels down, the Grout waits. On each level, solve the puzzles to open the grille over the winze (they're listed top left and marked on your map), then climb down together. Lava burns: jump the gaps. Deep water slows you to a swim. Each mine has a seed: type it in to go down the same one again. Chests hold relics that change how you play for the rest of the run. If you fall, a friend can pick you up. If everyone falls, the run starts again with a new mine.
